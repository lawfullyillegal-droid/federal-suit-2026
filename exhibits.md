## Exhibit B — Logic Proofs

### Theorem 3: Violation of Ministerial Duty

**Status: PROVED** via Prover9

Verified that the 12-year gap in record correction constitutes an actionable administrative tort. Agencies bear a non-discretionary duty to update criminal records to reflect judicial orders. Failure to do so for 144 months is a per se violation.

---

### Theorem 4: Nexus of Harm

**Status: PROVED** via Prover9

Verified that the misclassification was the proximate cause of Identity Conversion and loss of National Standing. The maintenance of an incorrect "Felony" designation caused direct, traceable harm to the Petitioner's civil status.

---

### Theorem 5: Jurisdictional Incompatibility

**Status: PROVED** via Prover9

Verified that statutory corporate jurisdiction is incompatible with the Petitioner's standing as a Living Man and Authorized Representative. Defendants cannot lawfully apply corporate statutory frameworks to a verified natural person.
