# Case Narrative: Long-Term Status Holding & Record Omissions

## 1. Chronological Holding Period
* **The 144-Month Gap:** Administrative units maintained a structural "Felon" status classification against the Plaintiff from 2008 through 2020, despite an underlying judicial determination of a Misdemeanor.
* **Identity Impact:** This persistent misclassification effectively neutralized the Plaintiff's standard administrative standing during the holding period.

## 2. Docket Omissions Audit
* **Omission Count:** 1,068 independent instances of due process record failures verified through formal data checking.
* **Rule Violation:** Systemic failure to execute the mandatory ministerial obligation to record the Return of Service on active public dockets.
