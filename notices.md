# Formal Notices

This page contains formal legal notices sent to relevant parties as part of the federal civil rights case.

---

## Notice to LexisNexis Risk Solutions

**File:** `Notice_LexisNexis_Risk_Solutions.txt`

Formal notice served to LexisNexis Risk Solutions regarding their role in the maintenance and dissemination of incorrect criminal record data classifying the Petitioner as a Felon, contrary to the judicial order of Misdemeanor entered in the Ventura 2007 Case.

**Basis of Notice:**
- Violation of the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)
- Dissemination of inaccurate public records
- Failure to correct records upon notification
- Contribution to the Petitioner's civil harm and administrative incapacitation

## Notice to Mohave County

**File:** `Notice_Mohave_County.txt`

Formal notice served to Mohave County regarding their administrative failure to update and maintain accurate criminal classification records in alignment with judicial determinations.

**Basis of Notice:**
- Breach of ministerial duty (non-discretionary)
- 12-year failure to correct records (2008–2020)
- Contribution to Identity Conversion and Civil Death
- Violation of the Petitioner's constitutional rights
