# EXHIBIT A: FORENSIC DATA INTEGRITY AUDIT REPORT
**SUBMITTED IN SUPPORT OF PLAINTIFF'S CIVIL COMPLAINT**

### 1. SUMMARY OF TECHNICAL FINDINGS
This exhibit compiles the technical data logs and audit metrics tracking record-keeping anomalies within municipal systems across the target jurisdiction. The audit focuses on two main systemic vectors: clerical database omissions and long-term status misclassifications.

### 2. COMPLIANCE AUDIT: MONELL PATTERN & PRACTICE DATA
* **Audit Reference ID:** LI-MOHAVE-2026-001
* **Scope of Review:** Public court docket logs and entry processing histories.
* **Metric Omissions:** A total of 1,068 separate instances were verified where mandatory, non-discretionary clerical entries recording the execution of service (Return of Service) were omitted from the active record stream.
* **Legal Impact:** Under standard municipal liability frameworks (*Monell*), an omission count of this scale demonstrates a persistent, systemic custom and policy of record-keeping deficiencies rather than isolated clerical oversight.

### 3. HARDWARE CAPTURE & RECONCILIATION DISCREPANCIES
* **Audited Infrastructure Node:** Node ID 031332 (Imaging System Platform)
* **Hardware Type:** Centralized document processing systems utilizing standard network printer/scanner architecture (RICOH IM 4000 Series).
* **System Discovery:** Data-flow analysis shows that document output spools and automated imaging tracking records failed to properly reconcile with public-facing dockets, creating unverified, disconnected data gaps across lower and upper court structures.

### 4. HISTORICAL REFRESH LATENCY TIMELINE
* **Holding Intervener:** 144-Month Status Gap (2008–2020)
* **Log Error:** Database registries failed to ingest or reflect the correct misdemeanor final disposition within standard data-refresh windows, resulting in the long-term maintenance of an inaccurate felony status across tracking networks.
* **Enforcement Fallout:** The system-wide dissemination of this misclassified historical profile served as the direct technological trigger for the subsequent generation of erroneous warrant flags and invalid alerts during traffic stops within the region.
